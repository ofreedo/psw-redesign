A Red Oak wine-theme wet bar.

Detail / Wine Theme Wet Bar

Solid Red Oak, built around a wine country lifestyle

A full wet bar in Red Oak with integrated wine storage, built as furniture-grade cabinetry rather than a stock bar unit — matched to the rest of the home's woodwork so it reads as original to the house.

Bars are frequently built alongside a kitchen remodel or as part of a larger finish carpentry package — see Kitchens and Finish Carpentry for related built-in and paneling work, including a built-in bar with a paneled mirror ceiling.

How It Works

From idea to install.

A bar has to plan around plumbing, refrigeration, and glass storage before a single cabinet is built.

01

Consultation & Drawings

Walk the space, talk through plumbing, wine storage, and seating — leave with a real drawing.

02

Material & Layout Selection

Species and counter material selected to match the house; wine racking and appliance cutouts planned into the layout.

03

Shop-Built, Hand-Finished

Built and finished in the shop, coordinated with your plumber and electrician for rough-in requirements.

04

Installation

Set, leveled, and finished on-site — hooked up and ready to pour.
