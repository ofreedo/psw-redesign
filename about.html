The Beginning

Trained the old way, by a master.

Philip Snyder started working professionally with wood in 1987, apprenticing early in his career under Mark Schoettlin, a master woodworker. That apprenticeship set the standard Philip has held every project to since — an insistence on doing joinery the way it was meant to be done, not the way that's fastest.

Philip's passion for woodworking drives him to attain high quality craftsmanship and extreme loyalty to his clientele. Nearly forty years later, he's still the one at the bench.

The Approach

Old world hand tools, modern machinery.

PSW unites old world hand tools and modern machinery to execute old world joinery — mortise & tenon, dovetail, and other techniques that have outlasted every trend in cabinetry — to achieve a well-balanced blend of artistic form, function, and aesthetics.

PSW specializes in custom woodworking of all aspects and styles, from utilitarian cabinetry to furniture-grade cabinets and fine furnishings, plus custom fabrication and installation of fine finish & trim carpentry: wainscoting, coffered ceilings, paneling, pantries, closets, doors, windows, and moldings.
