About Philip Snyder

Nearly four decades at the bench.

Philip Snyder started working professionally with wood in 1987, apprenticing under master woodworker Mark Schoettlin. That grounding in old-world joinery — mortise & tenon, dovetail — still drives every piece that leaves the shop.

PS Woodworking blends traditional hand-tool technique with modern machinery to strike a balance of artistic form, function, and durability — from utilitarian cabinetry to furniture-grade fine woodworking and full architectural trim packages.
