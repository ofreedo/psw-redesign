Category 05 — Doors, Gates & Windows

Pegged mortise & tenon joinery — the kind that outlives the hinges.

Every door, gate, and window that leaves the shop is built with true mortise & tenon construction, pegged by hand — not glued butt joints dressed up to look traditional. It shows the first time you swing one shut.

A plain opening becomes a pegged mortise & tenon gate set into a full paneled entry — hand-built to carry decades of daily swing without sagging.

01 / Craftsman Entry

Mortise & tenon with exposed pegs

A true craftsman-style door built the way the style was originally built — visible pegged joinery, not applied trim pretending to be structural.

02 / Wine Cellar

Redwood door built from reclaimed wine barrels

Retired wine barrel staves resawn and rejoined into a solid Redwood door — a literal piece of the valley's wine history hung on new hinges.

03 / Garden Gate

A mortise & tenon gate with accent arbor

Built as a single structure with its own arbor overhead, so the gate reads as garden architecture rather than a fence interruption.

How It Works

From idea to install.

Doors, gates, and windows take daily structural stress and weather exposure — the process starts with species selection built for the specific opening.

01

Consultation & Drawings

Measure the opening, talk through style, hardware, and exposure — leave with a real drawing.

02

Species & Joinery Selection

Vertical-grain Fir, Redwood, or Alder chosen for weather exposure, then joined with pegged mortise & tenon — never a glued butt joint.

03

Shop-Built, Hand-Finished

Built and finished in the shop so the door or gate arrives ready to hang, not ready to warp on-site.

04

Installation

Hung, hardware set, and adjusted on-site until it swings and seats exactly right.
